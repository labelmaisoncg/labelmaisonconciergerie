FAQ

Frequently asked questions about sourcing from China

Another question? Message us on WhatsApp.

Ask my question on WhatsApp→

Do you work with investors based abroad?

Yes. We work remotely over WhatsApp: you present your property, your needs and your budget, and we review the sourcing options suited to your project.

Is it really cheaper to source from China?

Some products can be bought on competitive terms directly from manufacturers. However, the factory price is not the final cost. Shipping, insurance, customs formalities, taxes, inspections and installation must be included in the comparison.

Which products can be sourced from China?

We mainly look at kitchens, bathroom furniture, fittings, furniture, lighting and interior fit-out solutions, subject to their compliance with the requirements of the destination country.

Do you only work with property investors?

We mainly work with investors and owners who want to fit out or enhance a property. Second homes and high-end residences can also be considered.

Can you fit out an entire apartment?

Yes, we can review a complete project covering several product categories: kitchen, bathroom, furniture and fittings. The scope of our support is defined for each project.

How does delivery work?

Shipping, insurance, customs clearance and delivery terms are defined according to the products, volumes and contractual arrangements.

Do you also offer installation?

Installation can be arranged with qualified professionals. It is subject to a separate scope and quote depending on the work involved.

How do I get a quote?

Contact us on WhatsApp with a description of your project, the property’s location, the products you need and your estimated budget. We will then review your request.
